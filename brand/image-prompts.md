# AI image prompts for `src/assets/images/`

Every prompt below replaces one placeholder file. Generate at **1536 × 1024
(landscape)** unless the entry says otherwise, then crop to the size listed.

## Before you start

In ChatGPT, **attach two reference images to every prompt**:

1. `brand/unifac-logo.jpeg` — the crest, for colour.
2. The photo of the girls in uniform on the red carpet — for the uniform.

Then paste **Block A** (the house style) followed by the numbered prompt for
the image you want. Block A is the part that keeps all seventeen photographs
looking like one school on one day, so do not skip it.

## After you generate

1. **Look at the hands and the faces.** AI still gets fingers wrong. If a
   hand is wrong in an otherwise good image, regenerate rather than keep it.
2. **Check the uniform.** The stripes must be horizontal on the vest and the
   skirt must be checked plaid. If the model drifts into plain jumpers,
   re-attach the reference photo and say "match the uniform in the attached
   photograph exactly."
3. **Resize and compress** to the exact pixel sizes above before dropping
   them into `src/assets/images/`. Keep the filenames identical — the site
   imports them by name and the build will fail if one is missing.
4. **Keep the file sizes down.** Aim under 300KB each. Astro converts them to
   WebP at build time, but a 4MB source still slows the build.

## One caution worth reading

These are invented pictures of a real school. A parent looking at the site
will read them as photographs of Unifac. Two of them are riskier than the
rest and are worth replacing with real photos as soon as you can get them:

- **`signboard.jpg`** and **`head-of-school.jpg`** — these say "this is our
  gate, this is our Head." A made-up one is a claim about a real place.
- **`library.jpg`** and **`laboratory.jpg`** — only keep these if the school
  actually has a library and a science lab that look roughly like this. The
  video shows a real science laboratory with lab coats and titration
  equipment, so that one is safe. The library is not in the video, so check
  before you publish it.

Everything else — classrooms, assembly, break time, art, celebrations — the
video confirms, so a generated version of them is illustration, not invention.
