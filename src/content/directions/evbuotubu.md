Campus 2 is at **2 Ikwebor Street**, off the **2nd Power Line**, by
**Aigbangbe Junction** in **Evbuotubu Quarters**. Aigbangbe Junction is the
landmark to aim for: from the junction, take the 2nd Power Line and Ikwebor
Street is the turning the signboard stands on.
